Common questions

What contractors need to know.

What work does LL Brown perform?

LL Brown provides operated crane service, rigging, beam setting, structural steel erection, bridge construction support, pile driving, demolition, clearing, excavation, trucking, and heavy-equipment support.

Where does LL Brown work?

LL Brown is based in Scottsburg, Indiana and supports projects across Indiana and Kentucky, including the Louisville and Southern Indiana region.

Can LL Brown support bridge and roadway contractors?

Yes. LL Brown supports prime contractors with bridge work, structural steel erection, beam setting, pile driving, clearing and grubbing, crane mobilization, and related jobsite work.

Is LL Brown a DBE contractor?

Yes. LL Brown is a certified Disadvantaged Business Enterprise. Current certification documents and bidding codes are available upon request.
